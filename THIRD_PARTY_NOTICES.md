## Moon imagery

Credit: **NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio**.

- Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA). Scientist: Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).

- `assets/ldem_3_8bit.jpg`: LRO / LOLA elevation preview.
- `assets/moon-rendered.png` and the moon pixels in `lib/client.js` are derived

## Astronaut artwork

`assets/floating-astronauts-v1.png` was generated with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool. `assets/floating-astronaut-v2.png` is an AI-generated edit of
that image and is the artwork used at runtime. Generation and edit prompts
are included beside the images. These are decorative generated illustrations,
not photographs of real astronauts.

To the extent the project author holds rights in this generated artwork,
it is made available under the project's MIT license. No third-party logos
or endorsement are intended.
